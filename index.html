Glacier Glow is a series rooted in the landscapes, waters, and wildlife of Montana — painted to hold the quiet beauty of the places that stay with you.

From luminous water and mountain reflections to intimate wildlife portraits, each piece invites you to slow down and spend a little longer in the scene.

If you’re drawn to calm, place-based art with a strong sense of atmosphere, this series was made for you.
